# Cellar & Co. Customer Policies

## Age verification
All alcohol purchases require the account holder to be 21 or older. Age is verified from the date of birth on the account and again with a government ID at delivery. Orders cannot be placed, and deliveries will not be handed over, to anyone under 21 or anyone visibly intoxicated.

## Ordering
Orders can be placed online at cellarandco.example or by phone at (555) 010-2244. An order is "processing" until it leaves the warehouse, then "shipped", then "delivered". Customers can track orders under Account > Orders or by asking support with the order number (the letters ORD followed by five digits).

## Changing an order
Items and the shipping address can be changed only while an order is still processing. Once an order has shipped, it cannot be modified; the customer can refuse delivery or request a return after it arrives.

## Cancellations and cancellation fees
Orders that are still processing can be cancelled free of charge. Shipped orders cannot be cancelled. Kegs require 48 hours notice to cancel; later keg cancellations carry a $25 restocking fee. There is no fee for cancelling any other order before it ships.

## Shipping and delivery
- Standard delivery: 2-4 business days, free on orders over $75, otherwise $7.99.
- Express delivery: next business day if ordered by 2pm ET, $14.99.
- Store pickup: free, ready within 2 hours at our Rockville store.
We deliver to Maryland, Washington DC and Virginia only. An adult 21+ must sign for every delivery. Customers can save multiple shipping addresses under Account > Addresses.

## Returns and refunds
- Unopened bottles can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
- Damaged, corked or incorrect items are refunded in full regardless of whether they were opened; report them within 14 days of delivery.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after approval.
- Refunds up to $100 are approved automatically by support. Larger refunds are reviewed by a supervisor within 1 business day.
- Opened bottles that are not faulty, and kegs that have been tapped, are not refundable.
Customers can track a refund by asking support with their order number.

## Payments and invoices
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Apple Pay and Google Pay. We do not accept cash on delivery. Invoices are emailed after each order and can be downloaded under Account > Invoices. If a payment fails, the order stays processing for 24 hours so the customer can update their card.

## Accounts
Customers can create an account at cellarandco.example/signup, edit their profile under Account > Settings, and reset a forgotten password at cellarandco.example/recover. Deleting an account removes saved addresses and payment methods; order history is kept for 7 years for legal reasons. Cellar & Co. offers a Standard account and a Cellar Club membership ($49/year: free express delivery and 10% off cases). Customers can switch between them at any time under Account > Membership.

## Newsletter
Customers can subscribe or unsubscribe from the weekly newsletter under Account > Notifications or with the unsubscribe link in any email.

## Contacting a person
Live support is available 9am-8pm ET, Monday to Saturday, at (555) 010-2244, help@cellarandco.example, or Live Chat. Any customer can ask to be transferred to a human agent at any time. Complaints are reviewed by a supervisor within 2 business days.

## Product availability
Stock levels update hourly. When an item is out of stock, its expected restock date is shown on the product page, and customers can ask support for availability. We cannot hold or reserve items that are out of stock.

## Wine storage and climate
For long-term storage, keep wine at a steady 12-14°C (54-57°F); steadiness matters more than the exact number, and daily swings of more than a few degrees age wine faster. Keep humidity between 60% and 70% so corks don't dry out; above 80% labels can mould. Store cork-sealed bottles on their side, away from light and vibration. Screw-cap wine can stand upright. Most wine we sell is made to drink within a few years and doesn't need cellaring. Spirits should be stored upright, because high-proof spirit damages corks; they don't improve in the bottle. Beer should be stored upright, refrigerated (about 3-7°C / 38-45°F) and away from light, and is best within a few months; most beer does not age.

## Serving and opened bottles
Serve sparkling wine at 6-8°C (43-46°F), white and rosé at 8-12°C (46-54°F), light reds at 12-14°C (54-57°F) and fuller reds at 15-18°C (59-64°F). Once opened, re-cork and refrigerate: sparkling wine keeps 1-3 days with a stopper, white and rosé 3-5 days, red 3-5 days, and fortified wines such as port and sherry several weeks. Opened beer is best the same day.

## Product information we have
Product listings show the name, size, type, price and stock. We don't publish vintages, tasting notes, regions, producer details or scores, so support can't confirm a vintage or describe how a specific wine tastes. Customers who need those details can ask to be connected with our in-store staff.
